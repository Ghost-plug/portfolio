About

Built at the intersection of
numbers and pixels.

I'm Daniel Essien, a frontend architect who didn't start at a keyboard. My journey began with data analysis, spending years making sense of sprawling spreadsheets, user behaviour funnels, and KPI dashboards. That foundation changed how I build interfaces forever.

When I transitioned into frontend development, I brought something most engineers don't have which is an obsession with what the numbers say about the user. Every layout decision, every micro interaction, every CTA placement is backed by data, not instinct alone.

Today, I craft web experiences for businesses that want their product to feel as smart as it is. Clean code, considered design, and measurable impact and that's the standard I hold every project to.

When I'm not shipping interfaces, I'm probably reverse engineering a dashboard I admire, reading about cognitive load theory, or arguing that typography is UX.
